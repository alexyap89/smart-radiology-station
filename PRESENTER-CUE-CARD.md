# Presenter cue card — board session

**One page. Laptop fullscreen. One hand on Space, one on the pointer.**
Space / → advance · ← back · **Appendix** tab (top right, beside Restart) or **A** · Esc closes an enlarged film.
The clock starts when you leave the title screen and resets with **Restart** — it is your pacing check, not part of the story.

Open on the title screen and leave it there while you speak.

| Beat | Time | On screen | You do |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opening | 0:00–0:40 | Title — Smart Radiology | Say the opening. Do not touch the keys. |
| Transition | 0:40–0:55 | The "image them better and faster" line | Space once. One pause, then the line. |
| Four pillars | 0:55–1:15 | Four pillar cards | Space. Name the four, nothing more. |
| 1 · Detect earlier | 1:15–2:15 | Mr Lim's film, the call | Space. Let a board member call it, or call it yourself; click Yes/No; the overlay follows. |
| 2 · See better | 2:15–3:20 | CT, dual-energy tab, 3D model, denoiser | Space through. Click the fusion tab once; drag the model once. |
| 3 · Prepare better | 3:20–4:45 | Ms Tan, the phone, the room in 360° | Space. Let the room clip run a beat in silence. |
| 4 · Scan faster | 4:45–5:55 | AI-accelerated vs conventional | Space. Slide once. Pause on the slot arithmetic. |
| Close | 5:55–7:00 | Four programmes. One ambition. | Space to the summary. Say the close. **Hold the screen.** |

Space at the summary does nothing but toast — the deck cannot run past the close.

## If a demo misbehaves

| Beat | Fallback |
|---|---|
| Fracture call | Click the overlay's "skip" or press → — the AI result still shows, just without the guess |
| 3D drag | Leave the model still; the still frame reads fine |
| 360° room | Let it sit on the first frame; say the same line |
| Denoiser / accelerated-vs-conventional | The slider's default split already shows both sides |

## Appendix (only if asked)

- **Appendix** tab — click it from any screen; click again to return to the summary. The tab stays lit while you are inside. It holds the other tools and the key numbers only.
- **A** — same thing from the keyboard.
- `?appendix=1` — start with the appendix available.
- Appendix order: tools → key numbers by pillar.
- The unlock is per page-load: a reload always starts at the title screen, so a stray `?appendix=1` test cannot leave the talk starting in the appendix.
- "View the measured summary" opens a sheet over the close; Space closes it, then Space holds. Esc also closes it.

## The five answers they will ask for

Answer these yourself, in your words: AI safety (96.6% sensitivity, 99.2% NPV, 2.4% false positives, radiologist decides), the return (capacity, not cost saving), who overrides (the radiologist, always), staff cost (minutes of training), extra scans (none — every programme reuses or shortens a scan already commissioned).
