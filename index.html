AI denoising · 3D and 4D

Turn the AI denoiser up, and the dose comes down.

An AI denoiser rebuilds the image from the same raw data. The operator sets its strength: lower the dose, and the image stays readable. The dose saved pays for new dimensions of CT — a joint imaged repeatedly, in motion.

Smart Radiology · summary

What changed, and what it rests on

The station opened with one promise: patients imaged better and faster. This sheet is the measured version of that promise.

  1. Validate before deployment. 1,102 consecutive ED radiographs were audited with the AI output withheld from the reporting radiologist.
  2. Keep the radiologist in the decision. The tool flags; the radiologist interprets. AI caught 6 fractures the initial reports missed; radiologists caught 7 the AI missed.
  3. Answer the patient's question. Dual-energy CT settled an occult fracture the same night, and a 360° preparation experience got an anxious patient through her first MRI.

In one line: four programmes, each labelled for what it can currently prove — a validated fracture copilot, a live 3D and 4D lab, an initial evaluation on MRI acceleration, and a clinician-written preparation tool.
